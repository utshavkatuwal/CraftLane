# Craftlane — Product Overview

## Product

**Craftlane** — Real skills. Clear agreements. Better work.

Craftlane is a global freelancing marketplace that connects clients with
freelancers through transparent project scopes, evidence-based portfolios,
and structured milestone collaboration.

## Target Users

- **Freelancers:** Independent professionals (developers, designers, writers,
  consultants, etc.) who want to present evidence of their work and discover
  relevant projects.
- **Clients:** Businesses, startups, and agencies that need to hire capable
  professionals based on demonstrated skills and clear project agreements.

## Differentiators

- **Trust through evidence:** Freelancer profiles emphasize verifiable work
  samples, not self-reported ratings or fabricated testimonials.
- **Transparency before commitment:** Project scope, budget, deadline, and
  deliverables are defined before anyone applies.
- **Structured collaboration:** Milestones, deliverables, and revision
  workflows are managed within the platform.
- **No fake social proof:** No fabricated user counts, reviews, ratings, or
  activity metrics.

## MVP Boundaries (Phase 1)

The current implementation is a frontend-only prototype:

- Landing page, sign-up, sign-in, marketplace browsing, project detail,
  freelancer profile, client/freelancer dashboard shells, and 404 page.
- No backend is connected. Account actions and project interactions do not
  perform real operations.
- No fake data: no fabricated users, reviews, projects, or metrics.

## Non-Goals (for now)

- Native mobile applications
- Cryptocurrency payments
- Payments, escrow, or payout processing
- Production messaging or notifications
- Reviews and ratings
- Administrative moderation tools

These require separate backend and product work in later phases.
